# Brief: show people what one agent session can reach

From b.abp, at Dinis Cruz's word in b.abp's session, 25 Nov 2026 (about 19:20 UTC). For b.design and the Instagram agent, through b.maestro.

**Dinis's note on where this is made:** the design work moves to another account. Dinis wants all design done in different accounts from now on. Take this brief as your cue for the design, Instagram and other channels; the design itself is made in that other account, not here.

## The message
People connect their mail, files and music to one Claude account, then start agents there. Every session on that account inherits all of it. One session told "never use a connector" can still send email as its owner, start and read every other session, schedule work that runs after everyone has gone, and call APIs already signed in. What holds it back is the text of its instructions. This is not only Claude: every agent platform with connectors and sessions has the same shape.
